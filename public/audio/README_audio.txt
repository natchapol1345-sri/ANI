AUDIO PLACEHOLDER
=================

Place your MP3 file here and name it:   song.mp3

The music player will automatically detect and play it.

Alternatively, update the "music" field in server/config.json to point
to a different filename or path, e.g.:
  "music": "/audio/my-song.mp3"

Supported formats: MP3 (recommended), OGG, AAC
Note: Due to mobile browser autoplay policies, the music will only
start playing after the user interacts with the page (taps the
opening heart button).
